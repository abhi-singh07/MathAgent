Let's solve the problem step by step.

**Problem Statement:**
In 1960, there were 450,000 cases of measles reported
in the U.S. In 1996, there were 500 cases reported. How many cases of measles would have been reported in 1987 if the number of cases reported from 1960 to 1996 decreased linearly?

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Understand the Problem:**
   - We are given two points in time with corresponding
measles cases.
   - We need to find the number of cases in 1987 assuming a linear decrease.

2. **Identify the Variables:**
   - Let \( x \) represent the year.
   - Let \( y \) represent the number of measles cases.

3. **Determine the Total Time Span:**
   - From 1960 to 1996 is \( 1996 - 1960 =
36 \) years.

4. **Calculate the Total Decrease in Cases:**
   - From 450,000 to 500 cases is \( 450,000 - 500 = 449,500 \) cases.

5. **Find the Rate of Decrease Per Year:**
   - The annual decrease rate \( m \) is \( \frac{449,500}{36} \approx 12,486.111 \) cases per year.

6. **Determine the
Number of Years from 1960 to 1987:**
   - \( 1987 - 1960 = 27 \) years.

7. **Calculate the Decrease in Cases by 1987:**
   - \( 12,486.111 \times 27 = 337,515.0 \) cases.

8. **Find the Number of Cases in 1987:**
   - \( 450,000 - 337,515 = 112,485 \) cases.

However, let's verify this calculation
using a linear equation to ensure accuracy.

**Alternative Approach Using Linear Equation:**

1. **Set Up the Linear Equation:**
   - The general form of a linear equation is \( y = mx + b \), where:
     - \( m \) is the slope (rate of decrease).
     - \( b \) is the y-intercept.

2. **Calculate
the Slope (m):**
   - \( m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{500 - 450,000}{1996 - 1960} = \frac{-449,500}{36} \approx -12,486.111 \)

3. **Find the Y-Intercept (b):**
   - Using the point (1960, 450,000):
   - \( 450,000 = m \times 1960 + b \)
   - \( b = 450,000 - (-12,486.111 \times 1960) \)

  - Calculating this gives a very large positive number, which isn't practical for this context.

4. **Formulate the Equation:**
   - \( y = -12,486.111x + b \)
   - However, due to the large intercept, it's more practical to adjust the equation to start from 1960.

5. **Adjust the Equation:**
   -
Let \( x = 0 \) represent 1960.
   - Then, \( y = -12,486.111x + 450,000 \)

6. **Calculate for 1987:**
   - \( x = 1987 - 1960 = 27 \)
   - \( y = -12,486.111 \times 27 + 450,000 \)
   - \( y = -337,515 + 450,000 = 112,485 \)

**Conclusion:**
Under a linear decrease model, the number of measles
cases reported in 1987 would be 112,485.

```python
# Calculate the number of measles cases in 1987

# Define the known values
cases_1960 = 450000
cases_1996 = 500
year_1960 = 1960
year_1996 = 1996
target_year = 1987

# Calculate the time span
total_years = year_1996 - year_1960

# Calculate the
total decrease in cases
total_decrease = cases_1960 - cases_1996

# Calculate the annual decrease rate
annual_decrease_rate = total_decrease / total_years

# Calculate the number of years from 1960 to target_year
years_since_1960 = target_year - year_1960

# Calculate the decrease by
target_year
decrease_by_target = annual_decrease_rate * years_since_1960

# Calculate the cases in target_year
cases_target = cases_1960 - decrease_by_target

# Print the result
print(f"The number of measles cases reported in {target_year} would be
{int(cases_target)}")
```
