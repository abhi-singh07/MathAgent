     \]
     \[
     1 - p = \frac{1}{3} \quad \text{(probability of tails)}
     \]

Formula:**
   The probability of getting exactly \( k \) heads in \( n \) flips is given by:
   \[
   P(k) = \binom{n}{k} \cdot p^k \cdot (1-p)^{n-k}
   \]
   Where \( \binom{n}{k} \) is the binomial coefficient calculated as:
   \[
   \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}
   \]
