**Problem Statement:**
You have 5 marbles numbered 1 through 5 in a bag. Suppose you take out two different marbles at random. What is the expected value of the product of the numbers on the marbles? Answer as a decimal to the nearest
tenth.

**Key Idea:**
We'll use the concept of expected value, which is the average outcome if we were to perform an experiment many times. For this problem, the experiment is drawing two marbles and calculating the product of their numbers.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Determine the Total
Number of Possible Pairs:**
   - Since we're drawing two different marbles from five, the number of possible pairs can be calculated using combinations.
   - Formula: \( C(n, k) = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} \)
   - Here, \( n = 5 \) and \( k = 2 \).
   - Calculation: \( C(5, 2) = \frac{5!}{2!(5-2)!} =
\frac{5 \times 4}{2 \times 1} = 10 \) possible pairs.

2. **List All Possible Pairs and Their Products:**
   - The possible pairs (a, b) where \( a < b \) are:
     - (1, 2), (1, 3), (1, 4), (1, 5)
     - (2, 3), (2, 4), (2, 5)
     - (3, 4), (3, 5)
     - (4, 5)
   - Calculate the product for each
pair:
     - (1, 2): 1 × 2 = 2
     - (1, 3): 1 × 3 = 3
     - (1, 4): 1 × 4 = 4
     - (1, 5): 1 × 5 = 5
     - (2, 3): 2 × 3 = 6
     - (2, 4): 2 × 4 = 8
     - (2, 5): 2 × 5 = 10
     - (3, 4): 3 × 4 = 12
     - (3, 5): 3 × 5 = 15
     - (4, 5): 4 × 5 = 20

3. **Calculate the Sum of All
Products:**
   - Sum = 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 + 15 + 20 = 85

4. **Compute the Expected Value:**
   - Expected Value \( E = \frac{\text{Sum of Products}}{\text{Total Number of Pairs}} = \frac{85}{10} = 8.5 \)

### Final Answer:
The expected value of the product of the numbers on the two
marbles is \(\boxed{8.5}\).
