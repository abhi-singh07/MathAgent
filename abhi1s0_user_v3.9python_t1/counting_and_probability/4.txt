To determine the probability that \( ad - bc \) is even, we analyze the parity of the products \( ad \) and \( bc \). The expression \( ad - bc \) is even if both \( ad \) and \( bc \) are either even or odd. 

1. **Calculate the
probability that a product is even or odd:**
   - Each number has a 1/2 chance of being even or odd.
   - The product of two numbers is even if at least one is even, which happens with probability \( 1 - (1/2)^2 = 3/4 \).
   - The product is odd only if both numbers are odd, with probability \( 1/4
\).

2. **Determine the combined probabilities:**
   - Both \( ad \) and \( bc \) being even: \( (3/4)^2 = 9/16 \).
   - Both \( ad \) and \( bc \) being odd: \( (1/4)^2 = 1/16 \).
   - Total probability: \( 9/16 + 1/16 = 10/16 = 5/8 \).

Thus, the probability that \( ad - bc \) is even is
\(\boxed{\dfrac{5}{8}}\).

```python
from fractions import Fraction

# Calculate the probability that a single number is even or odd
prob_even = Fraction(1, 2)
prob_odd = Fraction(1, 2)

# Probability that a product is even or odd
prob_product_even = 1 - prob_odd * prob_odd  # 3/4
prob_product_odd =
prob_odd * prob_odd        # 1/4

# Probability that both products are even or both are odd
prob_both_even = prob_product_even * prob_product_even
prob_both_odd = prob_product_odd * prob_product_odd

# Total probability
total_prob = prob_both_even + prob_both_odd

print(total_prob)
```
