**Problem Statement:**
A traffic
light cycles through green for 30 seconds, yellow for 3 seconds, and red for 30 seconds. Leah randomly picks a three-second interval to watch. What's the probability she sees a color change?

**Key Idea:**
We'll determine the total time during which a three-second interval can include a color change
and divide it by the total cycle duration.

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Understand the Cycle:**
   - Green: 0 to 30 seconds
   - Yellow: 30 to 33 seconds
   - Red: 33 to 63 seconds

2. **Identify Color Change Points:**
   - Green to Yellow: 30 seconds
   - Yellow to Red: 33 seconds
   - Red to

3. **Determine Favorable Intervals:**
   - For each change point, Leah's interval must start between (change_time - 3) and change_time.
   - Total favorable time per change: 3 seconds
   - With three change points, total favorable time =
3 * 3 = 9 seconds

4. **Calculate Probability:**
   - Total cycle duration = 63 seconds
   - Probability = Favorable time / Total cycle duration = 9 / 63 = 1/7

**Final Answer:**
The probability that Leah sees a color change is \boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}.
