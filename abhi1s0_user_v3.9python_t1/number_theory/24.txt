To solve the problem, we first compute the squares of each integer from 1 to 10 and find their remainders when divided by 11. We then collect the distinct remainders, sum them, and divide by 11 to find the
quotient.

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Square each integer from 1 to 10:**
   - 1² = 1
   - 2² = 4
   - 3² = 9
   - 4² = 16
   - 5² = 25
   - 6² = 36
   - 7² = 49
   - 8² = 64
   - 9² = 81
   - 10² = 100

2. **Find each square modulo 11:**
   - 1 mod 11 = 1
   - 4 mod 11 = 4
   - 9 mod 11 =
9
   - 16 mod 11 = 5
   - 25 mod 11 = 3
   - 36 mod 11 = 3
   - 49 mod 11 = 5
   - 64 mod 11 = 9
   - 81 mod 11 = 4
   - 100 mod 11 = 1

3. **Identify distinct remainders:**
   - The remainders are 1, 4, 9, 5, 3.

4. **Sum the distinct remainders:**
   - 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 9 = 22

5. **Divide the sum
by 11 to find the quotient:**
   - 22 ÷ 11 = 2 with a remainder of 0.

**Answer:**

The quotient when m is divided by 11 is \boxed{2}.

```python
# Calculate the squares and their remainders modulo 11
remainders = []
for n in range(1, 11):
    square = n ** 2
    remainder = square % 11
   
remainders.append(remainder)

# Collect distinct remainders
distinct_remainders = list(set(remainders))

# Sum the distinct remainders
m = sum(distinct_remainders)

# Calculate the quotient when m is divided by 11
quotient = m // 11

print(quotient)
```
