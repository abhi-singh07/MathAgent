**Problem Statement:**
A shirt's sale price is marked \$14.40, which is 60% off the original price. How many dollars was the original price of the shirt?

**Solution:**

1. **Understand the Discount:**
   - The shirt is sold at 60% off the original price.
   - This means the
sale price is 40% of the original price (since 100% - 60% = 40%).

2. **Set Up the Equation:**
   - Let \( P \) be the original price of the shirt.
   - The sale price can be expressed as 40% of \( P \):
     \[
     0.40 \times P = \$14.40
     \]

3. **Solve for \( P \):**
   - To find \( P \),
divide both sides of the equation by 0.40:
     \[
     P = \frac{\$14.40}{0.40}
     \]
     \[
     P = \$36.00
     \]

4. **Verification:**
   - Calculate 60% of \$36.00 to ensure the discount leads to the sale price:
     \[
     0.60 \times \$36.00 = \$21.60
     \]
     \[
     \$36.00 -
\$21.60 = \$14.40
     \]
   - The calculation confirms that the original price is correct.

**Final Answer:**
The original price of the shirt was \boxed{36.00} dollars.
