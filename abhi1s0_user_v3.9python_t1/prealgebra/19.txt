**Problem Statement:**
Stan drove 300 miles in 5 hours, 20 minutes. Next, he drove 360 miles in 6 hours, 40 minutes. What was Stan's average speed in miles per hour for
the total trip?

**Key Idea:**
We will use the formula for average speed, which is total distance divided by total time. We'll break down the solution into clear steps.

### Step-by-Step Solution:

1. **Calculate Total Distance:**
   - First trip: 300 miles
   - Second trip: 360 miles
   - **Total
Distance = 300 + 360 = 660 miles**

2. **Convert Time into Hours:**
   - **First Trip Time:**
     - 5 hours, 20 minutes
     - 20 minutes = 20/60 = 1/3 hours ≈ 0.3333 hours
     - **Total Time = 5 + 1/3 = 5.3333 hours**
   
   - **Second Trip Time:**
     - 6 hours, 40 minutes
     - 40 minutes =
40/60 = 2/3 hours ≈ 0.6667 hours
     - **Total Time = 6 + 2/3 = 6.6667 hours**

3. **Calculate Total Time:**
   - **Total Time = 5.3333 + 6.6667 = 12 hours**

4. **Calculate Average Speed:**
   - **Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 660 miles / 12 hours = 55 miles per hour**

### Final
Answer:
\boxed{55}
