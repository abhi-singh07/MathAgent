   - Substituting \( x^3 \) into \( f(x) \) transforms \( x^4 \) into \( (x^3)^4 = x^{12} \).
   - Therefore, the degree of \( f(x^3) \) is 12.

   - When multiplying two polynomials, the degree of the
product is the sum of the degrees of the factors.
   - Thus, the degree is \( 12 + 10 = 22 \).

**Answer:**  
The degree of \( f(x^3) \cdot g(x^2) \) is \boxed{22}.
