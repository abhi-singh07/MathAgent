   - We have a quadratic equation: \(x^2 - 63x + k = 0\).
   - Both roots (\(p\) and \(q\)) are prime numbers.
   - We need to find how many possible values of \(k\) exist.

2. **Apply Vieta's Formulas:**
   - For a quadratic

x^2 - 63 x + k = (x - p)(x - q) = x^2 - (p+q)x + p \cdot q,
