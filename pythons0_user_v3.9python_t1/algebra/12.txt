### Step 4: Determine the Number of Years from 1960 to 1987

### Step 5: Calculate the Decrease in Cases by 1987
- **Total Decrease by 1987:** \(12,486.11 \times 27 = 337,125\)
cases

### Step 6: Find the Number of Cases in 1987
- **Cases in 1987:** \(450,000 - 337,125 = 112,875\) cases

### Final Answer:
The number of measles cases reported in 1987, assuming a linear decrease, would have been \(\boxed{112,\!875}\) cases.
