Solution: Let the side lengths of the rectangle be $a$ and $b.$ It follows that $ab=4(a+b).$ Expanding and moving all the terms to the left hand side, $ab-4a-4b=0.$ Adding 16 to both sides allows us to factor:
\[a(b-4)-4(b-4)=(a-4)(b-4)=16. \]From this point, the pairs $(a,b)$ that provide different areas are $(5,20),$ $(6,12),$ and $(8,8),$ and the sum of the possible areas is $\boxed{236}.$
