and product of its roots.

1. **Sum of the Roots**: According to Vieta's formula, the sum of the roots \(a + b\) is equal to \(-a\). This gives the equation:
   \[
   a + b = -a
   \]
   Simplifying this, we get:
   \[
   2a + b = 0 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
   \]

2. **Product of the Roots**:
Again, using Vieta's formula, the product of the roots \(a \cdot b\) is equal to \(b\). This gives:
   \[
   a \cdot b = b
   \]
   Subtracting \(b\) from both sides, we have:
   \[
   a \cdot b - b = 0
   \]
   Factoring out \(b\), we get:
   \[
   b(a - 1) = 0
   \]
   Since \(b \neq 0\), it must
be that:
   \[
   a - 1 = 0 \implies a = 1
   \]

3. **Finding \(b\)**: Substitute \(a = 1\) into Equation 1:
   \[
   2(1) + b = 0 \implies 2 + b = 0 \implies b = -2
   \]

4. **Verification**: Substitute \(a = 1\) and \(b = -2\) back into the quadratic equation:
   \[
   x^2 + x - 2 = 0
   \]
  
The roots of this equation are found using the quadratic formula:
   \[
   x = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 8}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm 3}{2}
   \]
   Thus, the roots are \(1\) and \(-2\), confirming that \(a = 1\) and \(b = -2\) are correct.

Solution: The given conditions imply that $$
x^2 + ax + b = (x-a)(x-b) = x^2 -(a+b)x + ab,
$$ so $$
a+b = -a \quad\text{and}\quad ab = b.
$$ Since $b \neq 0$, the second equation implies that $a=1$. The first equation gives $b=-2$, so $(a,b) = \boxed{(1,-2)}$.
