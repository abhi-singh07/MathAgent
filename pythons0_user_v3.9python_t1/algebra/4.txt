   - The original equation is \( y = |x| - 3 \).

   - **Two units to the left:** This shifts the graph horizontally. To shift left by 2 units, replace \( x \) with \( x + 2 \) in the equation.

   - **Three units

     \[
     y = |x + 2| - 3 - 3 = |x + 2| - 6
     \]

3. **Determine the New Vertex:**
   - The vertex form of an absolute value function \( y = |x - h| + k \) has its vertex at \( (h, k) \).
   - Comparing with \( y =
|x + 2| - 6 \), we rewrite it as \( y = |x - (-2)| + (-6) \).
   - Therefore, the vertex (minimum point) is at \( (-2, -6) \).

**Final Answer:**
The coordinates of the minimum point of the new graph are \(\boxed{(-2, -6)}\).
