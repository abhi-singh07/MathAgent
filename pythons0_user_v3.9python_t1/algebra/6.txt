Explanation:**

1. **Multiply the Ratios:**
   \[
   \frac{a}{c} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{b}{c} = \left(\frac{\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{21}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{135}}{\sqrt{8}}\right)
   \]

2. **Simplify Each Square Root:**
   - \(\sqrt{135} = \sqrt{9 \times 15} = 3\sqrt{15}\)
   - \(\sqrt{8}
= \sqrt{4 \times 2} = 2\sqrt{2}\)

3. **Substitute Simplified Forms:**
   \[
   \frac{a}{c} = \left(\frac{\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{21}}\right) \times \left(\frac{3\sqrt{15}}{2\sqrt{2}}\right)
   \]

4. **Multiply Numerators and Denominators:**
   - Numerator: \(\sqrt{10} \times 3\sqrt{15} = 3\sqrt{150}\)
  
- Denominator: \(\sqrt{21} \times 2\sqrt{2} = 2\sqrt{42}\)

5. **Simplify \(\sqrt{150}\) and \(\sqrt{42}\):**
   - \(\sqrt{150} = \sqrt{25 \times 6} = 5\sqrt{6}\)
   - \(\sqrt{42}\) is already simplified.

6. **Substitute Back:**
   \[
   \frac{a}{c} = \frac{15\sqrt{6}}{2\sqrt{42}}
   \]

7.
**Simplify \(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{42}}\):**
   \[
   \frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{42}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{6 \times 7}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}
   \]

8. **Rationalize the Denominator:**
   \[
   \frac{15}{2\sqrt{7}} \times \frac{\sqrt{7}}{\sqrt{7}} = \frac{15\sqrt{7}}{14}
   \]

**Final
Answer:**
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{15\sqrt{7}}{14}}
\]
----------------------------------------
Solution: We begin by realizing that $\frac{a}{c} = \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{c}$. Thus, we have $$\frac{a}{c} = \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{c} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{21}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{135}}{\sqrt{8}} = \sqrt{\frac{10}{21}} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{135}{8}} = \sqrt{\frac{10\cdot 135}{21 \cdot 8}}.$$We can then simplify by canceling common factors under the square root. $10$ and $8$ share a factor of $2$ while $135$ and $21$ share a factor of $3$, so we have $$\sqrt{\frac{10\cdot135}{21\cdot8}}=\sqrt{\frac{5\cdot45}{7\cdot4}}.$$We now simplify further and rationalize the denominator to get: $$\sqrt{\frac{5\cdot45}{7\cdot4}} = \frac{15}{2\sqrt{7}} = \boxed{\frac{15\sqrt{7}}{14}}.$$
