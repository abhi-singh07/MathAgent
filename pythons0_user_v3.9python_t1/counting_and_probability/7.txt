3. **Favorable Intervals**: For each change, Leah must start her interval between (change_time -

The probability that Leah witnesses a color change during her three-second interval is
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}.

**Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Traffic Light Cycle Duration:**
   - Green: 30 seconds
   - Yellow: 3 seconds
   - Red: 30 seconds
   - Total cycle: \(30 + 3 + 30 = 63\) seconds

2. **Identify Color Change Points:**
   - Green to Yellow: 30 seconds
   - Yellow to Red: 33
seconds
   - Red to Green: 63 seconds (or 0 seconds, as it's a cycle)

3. **Determine "Danger Zones" for Each Change:**
   - For a change at \(t\) seconds, Leah's interval must start between \(t - 3\) seconds and \(t\) seconds to include the change.
     - **Change at 30 seconds:** Start times from
27s to 30s
     - **Change at 33 seconds:** Start times from 30s to 33s
     - **Change at 63/0 seconds:** Start times from 60s to 63s

4. **Calculate Total Danger Zone:**
   - Each change contributes a 3-second window where Leah's interval would include the change.
   - Total danger zone: \(3
\text{ seconds/change} \times 3 \text{ changes} = 9\) seconds

5. **Compute Probability:**
   - Probability = (Total danger zone) / (Total cycle time) = \( \frac{9}{63} = \frac{1}{7} \)

Thus, the probability that Leah sees a color change during her three-second interval is \(\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}\).
