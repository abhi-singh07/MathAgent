\]

\[
z = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}

\[
z = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 - 4}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm
i\sqrt{3}}{2}
\]

Thus, \( z \) is a complex number, specifically a primitive 3rd root of unity. This means \( z^3 = 1 \), and the powers of \( z \) cycle every three terms.

**Step 2: Identify Periodicity**

\[
z^1 = z, \quad z^2
= z^2, \quad z^3 = 1, \quad z^4 = z, \quad \dots
\]

**Step 3: Compute \( z^k + \frac{1}{z^k} \)**

   \[

Solution: Since $z^2 + z + 1 = 0,$ $(z - 1)(z^2 + z + 1) = 0.$  This expands as $z^3 - 1 = 0,$ so $z^3 = 1.$  Then
\begin{align*}
z^4 &= z \cdot z^3 = z, \\
z^5 &= z \cdot z^4 = z^2, \\
z^6 &= z \cdot z^2 = z^3 = 1, \\
z^7 &= z \cdot z^6 = z, \\
z^8 &= z \cdot z^7 = z^2, \\
z^9 &= z \cdot z^8 = z^3 = 1,
\end{align*}and so on.  Thus, the powers of $z$ reduce to 1, $z,$ and $z^2,$ in cycles.

Also,
\begin{align*}
\left( z + \frac{1}{z} \right)^2 &= (z + z^2)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1, \\
\left( z^2 + \frac{1}{z^2} \right)^2 &= (z^2 + z)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1, \\
\left( z^3 + \frac{1}{z^3} \right)^2 &= (1 + 1)^2 = 4.
\end{align*}Since the powers of $z$ reduce to 1, $z,$ and $z^2,$ in cycles,
\begin{align*}
\left( z + \frac{1}{z} \right)^2 + \left( z^2 + \frac{1}{z^2} \right)^2 + \left( z^3 + \frac{1}{z^3} \right)^2 + \dots + \left( z^{45} + \frac{1}{z^{45}} \right)^2 &= 15 \left[ \left( z + \frac{1}{z} \right)^2 + \left( z^2 + \frac{1}{z^2} \right)^2 + \left( z^3 + \frac{1}{z^3} \right)^2 \right] \\
&= 15 (1 + 1 + 4) = \boxed{90}.
\end{align*}
