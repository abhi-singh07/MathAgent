**Problem Statement:**
Two circles, one of radius 5 inches, the
other of radius 2 inches, are tangent at point P. Two bugs start crawling at the same time from point P. The bug on the larger circle crawls at \(3\pi\) inches per minute, and the bug on the smaller circle crawls at \(2.5\pi\) inches per minute. We need to find how many minutes it will take before
their next meeting at point P.

---

### **Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Find the Circumference of Each Circle:**
   - **Larger Circle (Radius = 5 inches):**
     \[
     C_1 = 2\pi \times 5 = 10\pi \text{ inches}
     \]
   - **Smaller Circle (Radius = 2 inches):**
     \[
     C_2 = 2\pi \times 2
= 4\pi \text{ inches}
     \]

2. **Determine the Time to Complete One Full Revolution:**
   - **Time for Bug on Larger Circle (\(T_1\)):**
     \[
     T_1 = \frac{C_1}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{10\pi}{3\pi} = \frac{10}{3} \text{ minutes}
     \]
   - **Time for Bug on Smaller Circle (\(T_2\)):**
    
\[
     T_2 = \frac{C_2}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{4\pi}{2.5\pi} = \frac{8}{5} \text{ minutes}
     \]

3. **Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of \(T_1\) and \(T_2\):**
   - Convert \(T_1\) and \(T_2\) to whole numbers by finding a common denominator:
     \[
     T_1 = \frac{10}{3} \times 15 = 50
\quad \text{and} \quad T_2 = \frac{8}{5} \times 15 = 24
     \]
   - Now, find LCM of 50 and 24:
     \[
     \text{LCM}(50, 24) = 600
     \]
   - Therefore, the bugs will meet again at point P after **600 minutes**.

---

### **Final Answer:**

\boxed{600}
\]
