\[ 4n \equiv 2

\[ \frac{4n}{2} \equiv \frac{2}{2} \pmod{\frac{6}{2}} \]
\[ 2n \equiv 1 \pmod{3} \]

---

### Step 2: Solve the Simplified Congruence

Now, we need to solve:
\[ 2n \equiv 1 \pmod{3}
\]

To find \( n \), we can multiply both sides by the modular inverse of 2 modulo 3. The inverse of 2 modulo 3 is a number \( x \) such that:
\[ 2x \equiv 1 \pmod{3} \]

Testing values:
- \( x = 2 \): \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \equiv 1 \pmod{3} \)

Thus, the inverse is 2. Multiply both sides:
\[ n \equiv 2
\times 1 \pmod{3} \]
\[ n \equiv 2 \pmod{3} \]

---

### Step 3: Identify Valid Integers \( n \)

We need to find all integers \( n \) such that:
- \( 0 < n < 60 \)
- \( n \equiv 2 \pmod{3} \)

\[ 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29,
32, 35, 38, 41, 44, 47, 50, 53, 56, 59 \]

---

-

---
