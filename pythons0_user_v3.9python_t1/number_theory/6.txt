1. **Identify the Range**: Consider all two-digit numbers
from 10 to 99.
2. **Extract Digits**: For each number, represent it as 10a + b, where a is the tens digit (1-9) and b is the units digit (1-9).
3. **Calculate Sum and Product**: Compute the sum (a + b) and product (a * b) of the digits.
4. **Check Divisibility**: Verify if the number is divisible by

5. **Sum Valid Numbers**: Accumulate the numbers that meet both divisibility conditions.

# Initialize total sum
