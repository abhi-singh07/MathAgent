**Problem:** If the least common multiple of \( A \) and \( B \) is \( 1575 \), and the ratio of \( A \) to \( B \) is \( 3:7 \), then what is their greatest common
divisor?

**Solution:**

### Step-by-Step Explanation:

1. **Understand the Given Information:**
   - The ratio of \( A \) to \( B \) is \( 3:7 \). This means we can express \( A \) and \( B \) as:
     \[
     A = 3k \quad \text{and} \quad B = 7k
     \]
     where \( k \) is a common factor of
both \( A \) and \( B \).

2. **Recall the Relationship Between LCM and GCD:**
   - The least common multiple (LCM) of two numbers \( A \) and \( B \) is related to their greatest common divisor (GCD) by the formula:
     \[
     \text{LCM}(A, B) = \frac{A \times B}{\text{GCD}(A, B)}
     \]
   -
Given that \( \text{LCM}(A, B) = 1575 \), we can substitute the expressions for \( A \) and \( B \):
     \[
     1575 = \frac{(3k) \times (7k)}{\text{GCD}(3k, 7k)}
     \]

3. **Simplify the Equation:**
   - Calculate the numerator:
     \[
     (3k) \times (7k) = 21k^2
     \]
   - Since \( 3 \)
and \( 7 \) are coprime (they have no common factors other than \( 1 \)), the GCD of \( 3k \) and \( 7k \) is \( k \). Therefore:
     \[
     \text{GCD}(3k, 7k) = k
     \]
   - Substitute this back into the equation:
     \[
     1575 = \frac{21k^2}{k}
     \]
     \[
     1575 = 21k
     \]

4.
**Solve for \( k \):**
   \[
   k = \frac{1575}{21} = 75
   \]

5. **Determine the GCD:**
   - Since \( \text{GCD}(A, B) = k \), we have:
     \[
     \text{GCD}(A, B) = 75
     \]

### Final Answer:
\[
\boxed{75}
\]
