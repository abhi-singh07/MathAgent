**Problem Statement:**
A shirt's sale price is marked \$14.40, which
is 60% off the original price. How many dollars was the original price of the shirt?

**Key Idea:**
We can solve this problem using basic algebraic reasoning. The sale price represents 40% of the original price since 100% - 60% = 40%. We'll set up an equation to find the original
price.

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Understand the Relationship:**
   - The sale price (\$14.40) is 40% of the original price.
   - Let \( P \) be the original price.
   - Therefore, \( 0.40 \times P = 14.40 \).

2. **Set Up the Equation:**
   \[
   0.40P = 14.40
   \]

3. **Solve for \( P
\):**
   \[
   P = \frac{14.40}{0.40}
   \]

4. **Calculate the Original Price:**
   \[
   P = 36
   \]

**Final Answer:**
The original price of the shirt was \boxed{36} dollars.
