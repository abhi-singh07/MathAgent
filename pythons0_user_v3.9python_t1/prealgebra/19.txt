Problem: Stan drove 300 miles in 5 hours, 20 minutes. Next, he drove 360 miles in 6 hours, 40 minutes. What was Stan's average speed in miles per hour for the total trip?
 
----------------------------------------
assistant: <think>
First, I need to determine the total distance Stan drove by adding the two segments of his trip. He drove 300 miles and then 360 miles, so the total distance is 660 miles.

Next, I should calculate the total time he spent driving. The first part took 5 hours and 20 minutes, which is 5.333
hours when converted to a decimal. The second part took 6 hours and 40 minutes, converting to 6.667 hours. Adding these together gives a total time of 12 hours.

Finally, to find the average speed, I divide the total distance by the total time. Dividing 660 miles by 12 hours results in an average
speed of 55 miles per hour.
</think>

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Calculate Total Distance:**
   - First Trip: 300 miles
   - Second Trip: 360 miles
   - **Total Distance:** \(300 + 360 = 660\) miles

2. **Convert Time to Hours:**
   - First Trip Time: 5 hours 20 minutes
     - 20 minutes =
\(\frac{20}{60} = \frac{1}{3}\) hours ≈ 0.333 hours
     - **Total Time for First Trip:** \(5 + 0.333 = 5.333\) hours
   - Second Trip Time: 6 hours 40 minutes
     - 40 minutes = \(\frac{40}{60} = \frac{2}{3}\) hours ≈ 0.667 hours
     - **Total Time for Second Trip:** \(6 + 0.667 = 6.667\)
hours

3. **Calculate Total Time:**
   - **Total Time:** \(5.333 + 6.667 = 12\) hours

4. **Calculate Average Speed:**
   - **Average Speed:** \(\frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{660}{12} = 55\) miles per hour

**Answer:**
\boxed{55}
