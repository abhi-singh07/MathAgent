Let's break down the problem step by step.

**Problem Statement:**
Trey receives a 5% commission on every sale he makes. On the sale of a \$60 coat (before any
discounts), how many more cents will he receive if his commission is based on the original price of the coat rather than the price of the coat after a 20% discount?

---

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Calculate Trey's commission based on the original price:**
   - Original price of the coat:
\$60
   - Commission rate: 5%
   - Commission = 5% of \$60
   - Calculation: \( 0.05 \times 60 = 3 \) dollars

2. **Calculate the discounted price after a 20% discount:**
   - Discount rate: 20%
   - Discount amount: 20% of \$60
   - Calculation: \( 0.20 \times 60 = 12 \) dollars
   - Discounted
price = Original price - Discount amount
   - Calculation: \( 60 - 12 = 48 \) dollars

3. **Calculate Trey's commission based on the discounted price:**
   - Commission rate: 5%
   - Commission = 5% of \$48
   - Calculation: \( 0.05 \times 48 = 2.4 \) dollars

4. **Find the difference in
commission:**
   - Difference = Commission on original price - Commission on discounted price
   - Calculation: \( 3 - 2.4 = 0.6 \) dollars
   - Convert dollars to cents: \( 0.6 \times 100 = 60 \) cents

---

**Conclusion:**
Trey will receive **60 more cents** if his commission is based on the
original price rather than the discounted price.

```python
# Define variables
original_price = 60  # in dollars
commission_rate = 0.05
discount_rate = 0.20

# Calculate commission on original price
commission_original = original_price * commission_rate

# Calculate discounted price
discount_amount
= original_price * discount_rate
price_discounted = original_price - discount_amount

# Calculate commission on discounted price
commission_discounted = price_discounted * commission_rate

# Calculate the difference in commission
difference_dollars = commission_original -
commission_discounted
difference_cents = round(difference_dollars * 100)

print(f"Trey will receive {difference_cents} more cents.")
```
