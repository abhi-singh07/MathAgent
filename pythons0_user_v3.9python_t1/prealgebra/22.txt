**Step-by-Step Solution:**

Let's solve the problem using a
systematic approach.

**Problem Statement:**
- Each man danced with exactly 3 women.
- Each woman danced with exactly 2 men.
- Total men at the party: 12
- Find: Number of women at the party.

**Key Idea:**
We can solve this problem by setting up an equation based on the total number of
dances.

**Step 1: Calculate Total Dances from Men's Side**
Each of the 12 men danced with 3 women. Therefore, the total number of dances from the men's side is:
\[ 12 \text{ men} \times 3 \text{ women per man} = 36 \text{ dances} \]

**Step 2: Express Total Dances from Women's Side**
Let \( w \) be
the number of women. Each woman danced with 2 men, so the total number of dances from the women's side is:
\[ w \text{ women} \times 2 \text{ men per woman} = 2w \text{ dances} \]

**Step 3: Set Up the Equation**
The total dances from both sides must be equal:
\[ 36 = 2w \]

**Step 4: Solve for \( w
\)**
\[ w = \frac{36}{2} = 18 \]

**Conclusion:**
There are **18 women** who attended the party.

```python
# Calculate the number of women at the party
total_men = 12
dances_per_man = 3
total_dances_men = total_men * dances_per_man

# Let w be the number of women
# Each woman danced with 2 men, so
total dances from women's side is 2w
# Set up the equation: total_dances_men = 2w
w = total_dances_men // 2

print("The number of women who attended the party is", w)
```

**Answer:**
The number of women who attended the party is \boxed{18}.
