**Problem Statement:**
A 4-inch by 6-inch picture is enlarged by tripling its dimensions. A 2-inch-wide border is added around each side. We need to find the minimum number of linear feet of framing required to go around the perimeter of the
border. Framing is sold only in one-foot increments.

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. **Enlarging the Picture:**
   - Original dimensions: 4 inches (width) × 6 inches (length)
   - After tripling: 
     - New width = 4 inches × 3 = 12 inches
     - New length = 6 inches × 3 = 18 inches

2. **Adding
the Border:**
   - Border width: 2 inches on each side
   - Total addition to each dimension:
     - Width: 2 inches (left) + 2 inches (right) = 4 inches
     - Length: 2 inches (top) + 2 inches (bottom) = 4 inches
   - Final dimensions with border:
     - Total width = 12 inches + 4 inches = 16
inches
     - Total length = 18 inches + 4 inches = 22 inches

3. **Calculating the Perimeter:**
   - Perimeter formula: 2 × (length + width)
   - Perimeter = 2 × (16 inches + 22 inches) = 2 × 38 inches = 76 inches

4. **Converting Inches to Feet:**
   - 1 foot = 12 inches
   - 76 inches ÷ 12
inches/foot ≈ 6.333 feet

5. **Determining the Minimum Framing Needed:**
   - Since framing is sold in one-foot increments, we round up to the next whole number.
   - Minimum framing required = 7 feet

**Final Answer:**
The minimum number of linear feet of framing that must be purchased is \boxed{7}
feet.
