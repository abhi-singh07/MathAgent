**Key Idea:**  
This problem involves calculating the perimeter of an enlarged picture with a border and determining the minimum framing needed.
We'll break it down into steps and use Python to handle calculations.

---

**Step-by-Step Plan:**

1. **Calculate the Enlarged Picture Dimensions:**
   - Original dimensions: 4 inches by 6 inches.
   - Enlarged by tripling: 
     - Length = 6 inches * 3 = 18 inches.
     - Width = 4 inches * 3 = 12
inches.

2. **Add the Border:**
   - Border width: 2 inches on each side.
   - Total addition to length: 2 * 2 = 4 inches.
   - Total addition to width: 2 * 2 = 4 inches.
   - Final dimensions with border:
     - Length = 18 + 4 = 22 inches.
     - Width = 12 + 4 = 16 inches.

3. **Calculate the
Perimeter:**
   - Perimeter formula: 2 * (Length + Width).
   - Perimeter = 2 * (22 + 16) = 2 * 38 = 76 inches.

4. **Convert Inches to Feet:**
   - 1 foot = 12 inches.
   - Feet required = 76 / 12 ≈ 6.333 feet.

5. **Determine Minimum Framing Needed:**
   - Framing is sold in 1-foot increments.
  
- Since 6 feet is insufficient (6 * 12 = 72 inches), we round up to 7 feet.

---

**Python Code:**

```python
# Define variables
original_length = 6  # in inches
original_width = 4   # in inches
enlargement_factor = 3
border_width = 2     # in inches per side

# Step 1: Calculate enlarged
dimensions
enlarged_length = original_length * enlargement_factor
enlarged_width = original_width * enlargement_factor

# Step 2: Add border
total_length = enlarged_length + (border_width * 2)
total_width = enlarged_width + (border_width * 2)

# Step 3: Calculate perimeter in inches
perimeter_inches
= 2 * (total_length + total_width)

# Step 4: Convert inches to feet
feet_required = perimeter_inches / 12

# Step 5: Round up to nearest whole number
import math
min_framing_feet = math.ceil(feet_required)

print("Minimum framing needed:", min_framing_feet,
"feet")
```
